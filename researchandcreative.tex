\section{Applied information science for reusable research}
My research brings computational methods and publishing practices together to make scientific information easier to find, assess and reuse. I study how to retain information about sources, connect research tools and keep resources available. Genomics is one setting for this work, alongside the library services and reporting tools described below.

\subsection{Continuing research}
\textbf{2025: publications supporting current work.} I co-authored \textit{AgBioDatabase Finder}, BALROG-MON and \textit{Guidelines for Gene and Genome Assembly Nomenclature}. These address finding research resources, repeating analyses and using consistent names. BALROG-MON was published February 19, before my February 25 K-State appointment.\evidenceref{P1} In 2026, my co-authored \textit{Genetics} article, \textit{Toward standardization in arthropod and biodiversity genome projects}, extended the collaborative standards work.\evidenceref{R1}

\textbf{2025--2026: AgBioData research coordination.} As co-principal investigator and K-State subcontract principal investigator, I helped coordinate the hybrid community meeting, budget and team during the September 2025--August 2026 reporting period. These responsibilities build on the previous year's meeting planning and are documented in the annual-report preview. Meeting-report work continues; submission acknowledgment and independent evaluation calculations remain to be retained.\evidenceref{R2}

\textbf{2026, start month not established: manuscripts in progress.} I contributed to revision of \textit{Future is FAIR} and the response to reviewers, alongside preparation of the AgBioData meeting report. Both remain work in progress, with individual author contributions to be refined for final review.\evidenceref{R1}

\subsection{Instruction and research software}
\textbf{May 2026: data-curation instruction and follow-up.} Workshop records identify May 1 and May 29 events. I supplied follow-up resources on May 29 and received positive participant feedback. This continues my 2025 Carpentries teaching and research-data support; individual teaching hours and attendance totals are not established.\evidenceref{P1}\evidenceref{D1}

\textbf{June 2026 onward: HAIL and reporting methods.} The publication-reporting request described in Directed Service led to HAIL and HAIL-local development, alongside Prairie Observatory dashboard views. September software changes distinguish missing information from observed values, show the coverage and quality of collected records, add information from Janeway publishing software and allow records to be processed again using updated rules. These changes make the source data easier to examine and check. Full production runs and final report figures still require validation.\evidenceref{K1}\evidenceref{S1}

\textbf{August 7, 2026 onward: Barcode Simulator.} I advanced software that compares simulated sequence data and produces graphs, adding distance matrices and then compressed sequence files, FASTQ output, records of run settings and storage checks in September. These changes help researchers inspect and repeat analyses. The simulated quality scores do not model a sequencing instrument's errors, and the work does not establish new biological findings.\evidenceref{S1}

\textbf{August 11, 2026: PathwayByWire.} The merged methods work measures how completely a biological pathway is represented, exports KEGG pathway data and provides an OpenAPI interface for other software. It does not measure gene expression or pathway activity. Across these projects, my contributions include research direction, design decisions and review, with collaborators and AI-assisted development.\evidenceref{S1}
