\section{Research, Scholarly and Creative Activity}
My research brings computational methods and publishing practices together to make scientific information easier to find, assess and reuse. I study how to retain information about where data came from, connect research tools and keep resources available. Genomics is one setting for this work, alongside the library services and reporting tools described in Directed Service. This work matters to the Libraries because it helps shape academic collections and guide how services evolve: the methods I test become the practices OPE recommends.

The distinction matters: not every biological computation is a study of scholarly communication, so I separate published standards and discovery research from the software and methods work that supports it. This theme is the intellectual-sustainability program the OPE landscape describes -- building a research agenda around open infrastructure, data publishing and evidence of impact. Awards and requests are labeled as such, and publication is documented separately from downstream use.
