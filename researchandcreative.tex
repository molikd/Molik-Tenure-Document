\section{Applied information science for reusable research}
My research strategy integrates computational science, informatics and publishing practices to make scientific information more usable. Provenance, interoperability and sustainable access connect this agenda to Libraries research support. Computational genomics is one domain in which I apply these methods, not the organizing definition of my program.
