\section{Integrating applied information science with library leadership}
My work connects library services and computational methods so that people can publish, preserve, find and reuse knowledge. The \href{https://github.com/molikd/Molik-Tenure-Document/blob/cdc3dd5d651ccda7ef97922f21e287e425277643/evidence/2026/ope-landscape-june-2026.pdf}{June 2026 Open Publishing Exchange (OPE) Landscape and Executive Summary}, which I prepared, brings that purpose together across open education, repositories, New Prairie Press and data services: make access practical, build services we can sustain, and show whom those services help.\evidenceref{L5} This connects my research, partnerships and mentoring to OPE's daily work; genomics is one setting in which I develop methods for that broader purpose. The account below follows the 2025 portfolio through September 15, 2026.

\subsection{2025 foundation and continuing responsibilities}
\textbf{February--December 2025.} As Department Head of the Center for Scholarly Publishing, now the Open Publishing Exchange (OPE), I established a foundation for coordinated publishing, open-education and research-data services. The April research-leadership presentation, July landscape document, Tea with CSP and OATI governance drafting supported that work. I also supported publishing-platform transitions and leadership development. These efforts underpin the 2026 work below.\evidenceref{P1}

\textbf{2025--2026.} My i5k and AgBioData leadership, founding editorial work and research publications continued into the current year. My 2026 \textit{Genetics} article extends the 2025 publications on scientific information reuse. AgBioData meeting, budget and team coordination continued through the 2026 reporting period.\evidenceref{P1}\evidenceref{R1}\evidenceref{R2}
