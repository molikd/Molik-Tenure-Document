\section{Integrating applied information science with library leadership}
My work connects library services and computational methods so that people can publish, preserve, find and reuse knowledge. In 2026, I brought that purpose into departmental planning, grant development, mentoring and research-publication reporting. This account extends the 2025 portfolio through September 15, 2026.

\textbf{February--December 2025: building a departmental foundation.} As Department Head of the Center for Scholarly Publishing, now the Open Publishing Exchange (OPE), I worked to connect publishing, open education and research-data services. My April research-leadership presentation, July landscape document, Tea with CSP discussions, Open and Alternative Textbook Initiative drafting and publishing-transition oversight established priorities that I continued in 2026.\evidenceref{P1}

\textbf{2025--2026: continuing research and professional service.} My i5k and AgBioData work connects researchers developing shared practices for managing biological data. In 2026, I continued meeting, budget and team coordination and co-authored a published \textit{Genetics} article on genome-project standards. These continue my 2025 publications, committee and editorial work.\evidenceref{P1}\evidenceref{R1}\evidenceref{R2}

\textbf{March--July 2026: pursuing research funding.} I received a \$13,417 ID3A seed-funding commitment for an agricultural research-data platform.\evidenceref{G1} I submitted FAIROS Genome Notes in April (withdrawn in June), FFAR on April 29, Ag-DEx in June (not funded in August) and K-FORGE in July. I led Ag-DEx with Gwen Sibley as co-project director and K-FORGE as principal investigator. The research section gives the requested amounts and remaining award uncertainties.\evidenceref{G1}

\textbf{April--May 2026: supporting research and learning.} My achievements record reports increased Textbook 2.0 fundraising and a large research-data consultation.\evidenceref{D2} I supplied data-curation workshop participants with follow-up resources in May.\evidenceref{D1} Execution of my Lead Research Officer agreement was confirmed May 1, adding proposal and partnership responsibilities to my departmental role.\evidenceref{L1} AgBioData recognized my professional service with its Mission and Leadership Award in April.\evidenceref{N2}

\textbf{June 2026 onward: planning services and explaining research.} I prepared the \href{https://github.com/molikd/Molik-Tenure-Document/blob/cdc3dd5d651ccda7ef97922f21e287e425277643/evidence/2026/ope-landscape-june-2026.pdf}{June OPE Landscape and Executive Summary}, now linked from StaffNet's department overviews. It connects open education, repositories, New Prairie Press and data services with staffing and funding plans.\evidenceref{L5} In response to a request from the Office of the Vice President for Research, I developed related publication-reporting tools: HAIL collects and organizes records, HAIL-local produces reports from locally stored data, and Prairie Observatory provides dashboard views. Preliminary reports are available; validation and evidence of their use in decisions remain underway.\evidenceref{K1} My June 17 Research Weekly article explained the serials crisis and received favorable feedback.\evidenceref{D1}

\textbf{June--July 2026 onward: supporting colleagues and students.} As Libraries Faculty and Professional Staff Vice Chair, I recommended changes to promotion-and-tenure guidance; the June 23 response confirmed revisions.\evidenceref{N1} In July, I advised Gwen Sibley on successive drafts of a William T. Grant letter of inquiry, and she described using my recommendations.\evidenceref{L3} I also helped colleagues refine three student positions, arrange mentors, coordinate recruitment and prepare onboarding and training.\evidenceref{L2}

\textbf{August--September 2026: developing new work.} I delivered a Sunderland operating-model workbook, advised Carol Sevin on grant development and began bringing colleagues together for an undergraduate STEM education proposal.\evidenceref{L4} I advanced Barcode Simulator and PathwayByWire research software alongside the reporting tools.\evidenceref{S1} I became a guest editor for the ecology/evolution collection and accepted AgBioData sustainability leadership.\evidenceref{N2}
