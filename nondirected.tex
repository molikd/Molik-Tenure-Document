\subsection{Data and infrastructure development}
Shared standards and identifiers only work if communities keep them alive, so this theme covers community governance of data infrastructure rather than institutional platforms. My role is both disciplinary and library-facing: the same standard work that lets agricultural genomics data interoperate also demonstrates the metadata, identifier and preservation practices OPE provides. These are contributions to community design, not implemented architecture or funded services. The community design work here mirrors the institutional platform work in Directed Service, applied at the standards level.
