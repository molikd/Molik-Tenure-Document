\section{Institutional and professional service}
\textbf{2023--2025 foundation; continued in 2026.} Through i5k and AgBioData, I work with researchers on shared practices for managing biological data. My earlier Standards and working-group service developed into i5k Executive Committee and Training Working Group leadership and AgBioData Steering Committee service in 2025. I continued these roles in 2026, connecting library expertise in publishing, identifiers and data care with research communities. AgBioData's funded meeting work is described in the research section.\evidenceref{P1}\evidenceref{N2}

\textbf{2025: institutional and editorial foundation.} My cumulative record includes Environmental Health and Safety and Wildcat Scholar committee service, an advisory role with the Bioeconomy Information Sharing and Analysis Center (BIO-ISAC) and founding editorial work for \textit{Cyberbiosecurity Quarterly}. The 2025 portfolio describes these activities; the following entries focus on new work in 2026.\evidenceref{P1}

\textbf{February--March 2026: community and review work.} My achievements inventory records a February i5k Training Committee presentation and a March Genome Notes discussion. It also records four article-review and six meta-review assignments for the First International Workshop on Cyberbiosecurity on March 27. These are ten assignments, which may concern overlapping manuscripts. My PCI Genomics recommendation is additional editorial service, rather than an authored research article.\evidenceref{N2}

\textbf{April 2026: professional recognition.} I received AgBioData's Mission and Leadership Award, following its 2024 Data FAIRification Award and my continuing service to the community.\evidenceref{N2}

\textbf{June 2026 onward: Libraries Faculty and Professional Staff service.} As Vice Chair, I recommended clearer definitions of external reviewers and clearer treatment of internal evaluative statements in promotion-and-tenure guidance. Margaret Mohr-Schroeder confirmed revisions on June 23; the policy's effective date remains unconfirmed. On July 1, I advised Conner Dillard on representation, recruitment and wording for the ad hoc committee's proposed charge.\evidenceref{N1}

\textbf{August 14, 2026: editing a new collection.} I became a guest editor for the launched \textit{Cyberinfrastructure and Data Management in Ecology and Evolution} collection in \textit{Ecology and Evolution}. It invites research on the computing and data services that support the field, with submissions open through August 31, 2027. The collection is underway.\evidenceref{N2}

\textbf{September 11, 2026: new community responsibilities.} I became co-chair of the AgBioData Sustainability Working Group and accepted a future Plant and Animal Genome conference workshop co-organizer role. These appointments add responsibility for sustaining community resources and organizing opportunities to share that work; workshop delivery remains ahead.\evidenceref{N2}
