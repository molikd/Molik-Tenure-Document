\section{Building research support that can be sustained}
I propose the following priorities for discussion with my supervisor, building on the 2026 work and OPE landscape. They depend on agreed responsibilities and available staffing and funding.

\subsection{Directed Service}
\textbf{2027 onward: student and staff support.} I will work with supervisors to improve student mentoring and onboarding, keep training materials and collect feedback about responsibilities and learning. Over the five-year period, I will use what we learn to improve how we share work and prepare colleagues to take it on.

\textbf{2027--2031: reliable publication reporting.} I will work with the Office of the Vice President for Research and collaborators to validate a consistent report, explain its sources and limitations and collect user feedback. The goal is a reporting service with clear responsibility for maintenance and updates. We will document how people use the reports before claiming that they influence decisions.

\textbf{2027--2031: developing proposals and partnerships.} I will prepare the Sunderland Advanced Technological Education proposal for the next application cycle and strengthen partnerships. The Improving Undergraduate STEM Education planning process will help us refine the research question and each collaborator's responsibilities. For OAK-AI and other proposed services, I will seek agreement on the work and commitments before implementation.

\subsection{Non-Directed Service}
\textbf{2027--2031: professional service.} I will contribute to Libraries Faculty and Professional Staff work on policies and procedures and document the changes resulting from my recommendations. Through i5k and AgBioData, I will support training and work to sustain shared resources. I will carry out the planned workshop and ecology/evolution editorial responsibilities and assess where my contributions are most useful.

\subsection{Research, Scholarly and Creative Activity}
\textbf{2027--2031: publications, software and funding.} I will revise and submit the current manuscripts, identifying my contributions and recording their final status. I will continue developing research software that others can inspect and run, using tests and external feedback to assess it. Future proposals will build on the Ag-DEx review and other 2026 work; I will record submissions, awards and revisions separately.

\textbf{Annual review, 2027--2031.} I will continue learning about library science and assessment and apply that learning to my work. Before each annual review, I will update the evidence register, discuss progress with my supervisor and adjust these goals to reflect responsibilities and resources.
