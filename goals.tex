\section{Developing a sustainable research-support program}
Building on 2026 work, I propose the following five-year priorities for discussion with my supervisor. The immediate next-year milestones connect longer-term goals to evidence that can be reviewed; they do not imply approved staffing, funding or workload changes.

\subsection{Directed Service}
\textbf{2027 onward.} During 2027, I will refine the OPE student mentoring and onboarding pathway with supervisors, retain training materials and document feedback on role clarity and learning. Over the five-year period, I will use that evidence to improve delegation, continuity and staff development rather than measuring success only through recruitment counts.

\textbf{2027--2031.} I will work with OVPR and collaborators to validate a consistent KPI snapshot, record source coverage and known limitations, and document delivery and user feedback before describing institutional decision impact. The longer-term goal is a maintainable reporting service with agreed ownership, refresh expectations and interpretable indicators.

\textbf{2027--2031.} I will develop the Sunderland ATE concept for the next application cycle, strengthen partnerships, and use the IUSE planning process to refine the research question and responsibilities. OAK-AI and other proposed services will progress through agreed scopes and documented commitments before being presented as implemented programs.

\subsection{Non-Directed Service}
\textbf{2027--2031.} I will contribute to LFPS governance with clear records of recommendations and resulting changes, while distinguishing draft procedures from effective policy. Through i5k and AgBioData, I will advance training and sustainability work, retain selected outputs and evaluate where my expertise is most useful. Future workshop organization and the ecology/evolution special collection will be documented through completed contributions, not appointments alone.

\subsection{Research, Scholarly and Creative Activity}
\textbf{2027--2031.} I will advance the current manuscripts through revision and appropriate submission, documenting individual contributions and final status. I will continue developing reproducible research software, connecting releases and tests to methodological questions and external use where available. Grant development will build on the Ag-DEx review and other 2026 proposals, with distinct records for submissions, awards and revisions.

\textbf{Annual review, 2027--2031.} Throughout this period, I will strengthen my library-science and assessment knowledge and connect it to practice. Before each annual review, I will update the evidence register, assess progress with my supervisor and revise goals to reflect responsibilities and available resources.
