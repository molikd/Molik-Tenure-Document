\section{Five-Year Goals, 2027--2031}
The 2025 portfolio set nine commitments across directed service, non-directed service and research and creative activity. Each goal below restates its 2025 pledge, records what has happened since, and states the concrete outcome I intend to reach by 2031. Targets and dates remain proposals for discussion with my supervisor. Every goal names the result and how it connects to the wider Libraries, so progress is measurable rather than a description of activity.

\subsection{Directed Service}
\textbf{2025 pledge: carry out the CSP 2025--2030 strategic plan and grow New Prairie Press monographs.} The 2025 landscape and the June 2026 Open Publishing Exchange (OPE) landscape now guide planning, and the department has been reorganized around publishing, open educational resources and research-data services.\evidenceref{P1}\evidenceref{L5} \emph{Intended outcome:} by 2031, implement the landscape's four service lines as operating services with defined staffing and budget, and grow New Prairie Press monographs year over year against targets the revised textbook-initiative committee sets. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} these services carry the Libraries' scholarly-communication mission and give faculty a supported path to publish.

\textbf{2025 pledge: build a FAIR-aligned institutional data repository and serve as Library liaison to the Office of Research on CRIS/RIMS, with at least two joint initiatives.} Planning with the Office of Research and Research IT has continued, including repository discussions and publication-reporting work.\evidenceref{K1} \emph{Intended outcome:} by 2029, select and deploy a production research-data repository (Figshare, Dataverse, InvenioRDM or equivalent), have at least two documented Library--research-office initiatives in use, and align the service with federal public-access requirements. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} a working repository makes the Libraries the campus steward of research data rather than only a borrower of others' systems.

\textbf{2025 pledge: build a Kansas library publishing partnership of at least five institutions meeting monthly with 20\% cost reduction; stay engaged with the Janeway/Fulcrum coalition; add two science journals.} Press migration toward Fulcrum and Janeway continued, and I assessed Pressbooks--Canvas and Figshare sustainability.\evidenceref{P1}\evidenceref{D1} \emph{Intended outcome:} by 2029, formalize a Kansas publishing partnership with at least five signed members and a documented shared-cost reduction, and add at least two science titles where demand and capacity allow. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} shared infrastructure lowers the cost of open publishing across Kansas and keeps K-State at the center of that coalition.

\textbf{2025 pledge: sustain OPE staffing and mentoring so services do not depend on any one person.} I helped design an applied-learning pathway, coordinated three student positions and proposed paid training for student supervisors.\evidenceref{L2} \emph{Intended outcome:} by 2028, each OPE service line has a documented workflow, a named primary and backup owner, and an onboarding path for students and staff. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} operational continuity is what lets these services persist across staff changes.

\textbf{2025 pledge: continue my own library-science development.} I completed leadership and assessment programs and apply that learning directly to OPE planning.\evidenceref{P1} \emph{Intended outcome:} complete one advanced library-leadership or assessment program each review cycle and apply it to a named OPE or Libraries initiative. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} my development translates into services the Libraries can rely on.

\subsection{Non-Directed Service}
\textbf{2025 pledge: at least three grant submissions as co-investigator and at least two collaborations outside the library.} I contributed to coordinated multi-institution proposals and programs, though whether three submissions were completed is not yet verified against proposal records.\evidenceref{G1}\evidenceref{R2} \emph{Intended outcome:} by 2031, complete at least three co-investigator submissions and at least two active collaborations outside the Libraries, prioritizing awards over submission counts. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} external collaborations bring funding and visibility back to K-State services.

\textbf{2025 pledge: support onboarding of graduate assistants and staff and run at least two scholarly-communication workshops per year.} The OPE student cohort and applied-learning pathway now structure onboarding, and Carpentries instruction plus the May 2026 data-curation follow-up continued the workshop commitment.\evidenceref{L2}\evidenceref{D1} \emph{Intended outcome:} sustain at least two scholarly-communication workshops per year and mature the onboarding pathway so every student and new staff member has a defined role and mentor. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} skilled people are the Libraries' capacity.

\textbf{2025 pledge: keep AgBioData, BIO-ISAC and i5k leadership roles, attend a yearly conference, and publish two scholarly-communication journal articles.} All three leadership roles continue with expanded responsibilities, including AgBioData sustainability co-chair.\evidenceref{N2} \emph{Intended outcome:} hold these national governance roles through 2031 and publish at least two scholarly-communication articles across the period. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} national service gives the Libraries a voice in the standards its services depend on.

\subsection{Research, Scholarly and Creative Activity}
\textbf{2025 pledge: at least one peer-reviewed article or white paper per year on open publishing infrastructure, FAIR implementation and computational methods.} Three peer-reviewed articles appeared in 2025 with a fourth, on genome reporting standards, in 2026.\evidenceref{R1} \emph{Intended outcome:} publish at least one peer-reviewed article or white paper each year through 2031 on publishing infrastructure, FAIR implementation and research methods. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} this research is the evidence base for the services OPE recommends.

\textbf{2025 pledge: launch and direct \textit{Cyberbiosecurity Quarterly}, build the board and seek indexing by 2028.} The journal has launched with editorial governance and peer-review workflows in place, and I have editorial leadership.\evidenceref{N2} \emph{Intended outcome:} grow the editorial board and submissions and achieve indexing and visibility by 2028. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} a successful journal demonstrates New Prairie Press as a credible scholarly publisher.

\textbf{2025 pledge: continue GAAN and FHR metadata development plus at least one software tool, and co-author at least two whitepapers.} Open-tool and metadata-standards work continues through the portfolio's reproducible software, including Barcode Simulator, PathwayByWire and the HAIL reporting tools.\evidenceref{S1} \emph{Intended outcome:} ship at least one maintained tool release tied to a methodological question and co-author at least two whitepapers on preservation, retention policy and artificial-intelligence-augmented metadata workflows. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} maintained tools and documented methods become reusable infrastructure for library services.

\textbf{Build the scholarly-communication research program and the undergraduate-publishing research agenda.} Several manuscripts, reporting tools and partnerships are in progress, and course and study concepts exist, but the UPP's research-lab expectation and the undergraduate publishing/student-outcomes line are not yet established.\evidenceref{R2}\evidenceref{S1} \emph{Intended outcome:} by 2029, establish a functioning scholarly-communication research program with retained evidence of impact, and by 2031 produce at least one completed undergraduate-publishing or student-outcomes study with published results. \emph{Connection to the Libraries:} this research tells the Libraries how their collections and services should evolve, which is the core contribution of library research leadership.
