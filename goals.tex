\section{Five-year goals, 2027--2031}
The nine 2025 pledges below are retained as single-line continuity anchors;
each goal states the intended outcome that follows from it. Two further goals
are new commitments, labeled as such rather than as 2025 pledges. Every goal
gives a specific, measurable outcome with a target date, explains why the
outcome matters to the Libraries, and names the sustainability dimension it
advances -- financial, operational, technical, or intellectual, as defined in
the Statement of Accomplishments. The 2027--2031 window spans a standard five
years from this review; the mid-probation review in December 2027 and the
final tenure review in December 2029 fall within it as checkpoints. Targets
and dates remain proposals for discussion with my supervisor, not an approved
performance agreement.

\subsection{Directed Service}
\textbf{Goal 1. Carry out the publishing-exchange strategic plan and grow the press.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} carry out the Center for Scholarly Publishing (CSP) 2025--2030 strategic plan and grow New Prairie Press monographs. By 2031, complete the plan priorities assigned to the Open Publishing Exchange (OPE) across publishing infrastructure, open educational resources, and research-data services, and sustain year-on-year growth in New Prairie Press monograph output with annual counts reported. This is how the Libraries take part in creating, publishing, sharing, and preserving scholarship rather than only collecting it. \textit{Sustainability: financial and operational.}

\textbf{Goal 2. Deploy a production research-data repository with the research office.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} build a FAIR-aligned institutional data repository and serve as Library liaison to the Office of Research on research information systems (Current Research Information Systems / Research Information Management Systems, CRIS/RIMS), with at least two joint initiatives. By 2029, select and deploy a production research-data repository (Figshare, Dataverse, InvenioRDM, or equivalent), complete at least two documented Library--research-office initiatives in use, and align the service with federal public-access requirements. This makes the Libraries the campus steward of research data rather than a borrower of others' systems. \textit{Sustainability: technical and financial.}

\textbf{Goal 3. Formalize shared publishing partnerships and add science titles.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} build a Kansas library publishing partnership of at least five institutions meeting monthly with 20\% cost reduction; stay engaged with the Janeway/Fulcrum coalition; add two science journals. By 2030, formalize a Kansas library publishing partnership of at least five institutions with regular coordination and shared-cost savings measured against the 20\% target, sustain coalition engagement, and add two science journals where demand and capacity allow. Shared infrastructure lowers publishing costs for every member library and gives K-State authors dependable open venues. \textit{Sustainability: financial and technical.}

\textbf{Goal 4. New commitment: make OPE services independent of any one person.} By 2028, establish documented roles, named-mentor coverage, and cross-trained backup for each core OPE service, with published onboarding and succession expectations. Services that survive turnover protect the Libraries' investment in them and let growth continue without depending on unsupported individual effort. \textit{Sustainability: operational.}

\subsection{Non-Directed Service}
\textbf{Goal 5. Sustain grant collaboration beyond the Libraries.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} at least three grant submissions as co-investigator and at least two collaborations outside the library. Through 2031, join at least three grant submissions as co-investigator and sustain at least two active collaborations outside the Libraries, prioritizing awards and jointly delivered outcomes over submission counts. Bringing library expertise in publishing, identifiers, and data care into funded research connects library services to durable support and extends the network that sustains them. \textit{Sustainability: financial and intellectual.}

\textbf{Goal 6. Sustain onboarding and scholarly-communication training.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} support onboarding of graduate assistants and staff; run at least two scholarly-communication workshops per year. Through 2031, maintain the OPE onboarding pathway with supervisors and deliver at least two scholarly-communication workshops per year with current materials and documented feedback on role clarity and learning. A trained workforce with defined roles is what lets services continue and improve. \textit{Sustainability: operational and intellectual.}

\textbf{Goal 7. Keep national leadership roles and publish on practice.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} keep AgBioData, BIO-ISAC, and i5k leadership roles; attend a yearly conference; publish two scholarly-communication journal articles. Through 2031, retain national community leadership roles where my expertise is most useful -- AgBioData (agricultural genomics data standards), the Bioeconomy Information Sharing and Analysis Center (BIO-ISAC), and i5k (arthropod genomics) -- attend at least one conference per year, and publish two scholarly-communication journal articles. External governance gives the Libraries a voice in standards and early sight of where scholarly communication is heading, which feeds back into OPE services. \textit{Sustainability: intellectual.}

\subsection{Research, Scholarly and Creative Activity}
\textbf{Goal 8. Publish yearly on publishing infrastructure and methods.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} at least one peer-reviewed article or white paper per year on open publishing infrastructure, FAIR implementation, and computational methods. Through 2031, publish at least one peer-reviewed article or white paper per year on open publishing infrastructure, FAIR implementation, and computational methods. This research helps the Libraries shape their collections and guide how services evolve: the methods tested become the practices OPE recommends. \textit{Sustainability: intellectual.}

\textbf{Goal 9. Build the journal to indexed visibility.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} launch and direct \textit{Cyberbiosecurity Quarterly}; build the board; seek indexing and visibility by 2028. By 2028, grow the editorial board and submissions and achieve indexing and visibility for \textit{Cyberbiosecurity Quarterly}. Editorial leadership gives the Libraries a stake in how research is vetted, framed, and shared rather than only supporting publication after the fact. \textit{Sustainability: intellectual and technical.}

\textbf{Goal 10. Ship maintained tools and white papers on metadata futures.} \textbf{2025 pledge:} continue genome-assembly-nomenclature (GAAN) and metadata standards (FHR) development plus at least one software tool; co-author at least two white papers. GAAN and FHR are retained here as the project names used in the 2025 record. By 2030, ship at least one maintained software-tool release tied to a methodological question and co-author at least two white papers on preservation, retention policy, and AI-augmented metadata workflows. Maintained tools and shared standards keep agricultural and genomic data findable and reusable, which is the technical foundation OPE's data services build on. \textit{Sustainability: technical and intellectual.}

\textbf{Goal 11. New commitment: build a scholarly-communication research lab.} By 2030, establish a scholarly-communication research lab with a defined agenda, staffing, and partnerships, and within it complete at least one empirical study of undergraduate publishing participation or student outcomes by 2031. A standing lab turns isolated projects into a cumulative program that studies open infrastructure, data publishing, and evidence of impact -- and answers whether student publishing participation changes learning. \textit{Sustainability: intellectual and operational.}

\textbf{Annual review and continuing development, 2027--2031.} Before each annual review, I will update the evidence register, discuss progress with my supervisor, and adjust these goals to reflect responsibilities and resources. As a standing commitment reviewed each year, I will continue my own library-science and assessment development and apply that learning to OPE leadership. These goals remain proposals until my supervisor agrees; they are not an approved performance agreement.
