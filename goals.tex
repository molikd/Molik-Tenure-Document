\section{Goals continuing from 2025}
The 2025 portfolio set nine commitments across directed service,
non-directed service, and research and creative activity. Each goal below
starts from its 2025 pledge, notes what has happened since, and gives the
updated direction. Targets and dates remain proposals for discussion with my
supervisor, not an approved performance agreement.

\subsection{Directed Service}
\textbf{2025 pledge: carry out the CSP 2025--2030 strategic plan and grow New
Prairie Press monographs.} The 2025 landscape document and executive summary
now guide planning, and the Center for Scholarly Publishing has become the
Open Publishing Exchange.\evidenceref{P1}\evidenceref{L5} Through 2027--2031
I will carry out the plan's priorities -- publishing infrastructure, open
educational resources, and research-data services -- and sustain monograph
growth, adjusting year-on-year targets as the revised textbook-initiative
committee structure takes effect.

\textbf{2025 pledge: build a FAIR-aligned institutional data repository and
serve as Library liaison to the Office of Research on CRIS/RIMS, with at
least two joint initiatives.} Planning with the Office of Research and
Research IT has continued, including repository discussions and reporting
work on research data.\evidenceref{K1} Through 2027--2031 I will bring the
repository choice (Figshare, Dataverse, or equivalent) to a decision,
complete at least two joint Library--research-office initiatives, and align
services with federal public-access requirements.

\textbf{2025 pledge: build a Kansas library publishing partnership of at
least five institutions meeting monthly with 20\% cost reduction; stay
engaged with the Janeway/Fulcrum coalition; add two science journals.}
Press migration toward Fulcrum and Janeway continued under the publishing
program.\evidenceref{P1} Through 2027--2031 I will formalize the Kansas
partnership, stay engaged with the coalition, and add science titles where
demand and capacity allow, revising cost and meeting targets against actual
membership.

\subsection{Non-Directed Service}
\textbf{2025 pledge: at least three grant submissions as co-investigator; at
least two collaborations outside the library.} I have served as
co-investigator on coordinated multi-institution proposals and
programs.\evidenceref{G1}\evidenceref{R2} These roles show ongoing
collaboration; whether three submissions were completed has not yet been
verified against proposal records, so that count remains an open target.
Through 2027--2031 I will continue joint proposal development with faculty
at a comparable pace, prioritizing awards over submissions.

\textbf{2025 pledge: support onboarding of graduate assistants and staff; run
at least two scholarly-communication workshops per year.} The Open Publishing
Exchange student cohort and applied-learning pathway now structure onboarding,
and Carpentries instruction plus May 2026 data-curation follow-up continued
the workshop commitment.\evidenceref{L2}\evidenceref{D1} Through 2027--2031 I
will mature the onboarding pathway with supervisors, keep training materials
current, and document feedback on role clarity and learning.

\textbf{2025 pledge: keep AgBioData, BIO-ISAC, and i5k leadership roles;
attend a yearly conference; publish two scholarly-communication journal
articles.} All three leadership roles continue with expanded
responsibilities.\evidenceref{N2} Conference attendance and journal-article
output continue as commitments rather than completed counts.\evidenceref{R1}
Through 2027--2031 I will keep these roles where my expertise is most useful
and continue publishing on scholarly-communication practice.

\subsection{Research, Scholarly and Creative Activity}
\textbf{2025 pledge: at least one peer-reviewed article or white paper per
year on open publishing infrastructure, FAIR implementation, and
computational methods.} Three peer-reviewed articles appeared in 2025 with a
fourth, on genome reporting standards, in 2026.\evidenceref{R1} Through
2027--2031 I will maintain the yearly pace, focusing on publishing
infrastructure, FAIR implementation, and the methods behind the tools
described below.

\textbf{2025 pledge: launch and direct \textit{Cyberbiosecurity Quarterly};
build the board; seek indexing and visibility by 2028.} The journal has
launched with editorial governance and peer-review workflows in
place.\evidenceref{N2} Through 2027--2031 I will grow the editorial board
and submissions and pursue indexing and visibility on the original 2028
horizon.

\textbf{2025 pledge: continue GAAN and FHR metadata development plus at
least one software tool; co-author at least two whitepapers.} Open-tool and
metadata-standards work continues through the portfolio's reproducible
software, including GAAN development and FHR metadata work.\evidenceref{S1}
Through 2027--2031 I will ship at least one maintained tool release tied to
a methodological question and co-author at least two whitepapers on
preservation, retention policy, and AI-augmented metadata workflows.

\textbf{Annual review, 2027--2031.} I will continue learning about library science and assessment and apply that learning to my work. Before each annual review, I will update the evidence register, discuss progress with my supervisor and adjust these goals to reflect responsibilities and resources.
